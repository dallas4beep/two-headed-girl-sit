About

Have you ever seen these lesbian twins? Have you ever loved a nation as much as they love Puerto Rico? Probably not, but you're still invited to the party. Fusing indie-pop hooks with punk-rock fire, THG crafts the ultimate Queercore soundtrack for contemporary life. Their unique sonic DNA blends the alt-rock grit of The Breeders with the feel-good energy of a band from The O.C. soundtrack, transforming their deep love for community into anthems. Driven by a contagious live presence that guarantees a high-energy room, THG will have your crowd shouting 🇵🇷 WEPA 🇵🇷 and dancing until sunrise.
